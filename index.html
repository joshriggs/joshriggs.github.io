About

Designing products
people actually want
to use.

I'm a product designer with a decade of experience shipping digital products across consumer apps, developer tools, and enterprise software. I care deeply about the space between intention and experience — the gap between what a product is supposed to do and what it actually feels like to use.

My process is collaborative, iterative, and grounded in real user behavior. I work closely with engineers, PMs, and stakeholders to move from ambiguous problems to clear, considered solutions. I'm as comfortable running a research session as I am pushing pixels.

When I'm not designing I'm probably hiking, reading about typefaces, or arguing about coffee.
